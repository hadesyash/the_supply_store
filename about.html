Our Story

The Supply Store is a trusted one-stop destination for industrial supply solutions, dedicated to delivering quality, reliability, and value to manufacturers, contractors, and institutions. Based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, the organization is proudly woman-led, reflecting a leadership approach built on professionalism, integrity, and strong relationship management. We focus on dependable sourcing, fair pricing, and timely delivery to ensure our clients receive consistent and efficient supply for their industrial requirements.

As an authorized distributor of Addison Paints & Chemicals (a division of Simpson & Co. Ltd.), The Supply Store is built on strong supplier relationships and a commitment to high product standards. Our approach goes beyond supplying materials - we aim to provide dependable solutions backed by transparency, industry understanding, and a commitment to service excellence.

Driven by a customer-first mindset, we simplify procurement by bringing trusted brands and reliable solutions under one roof. With a focus on long-term partnerships, responsive service, and consistent quality, The Supply Store strives to be a preferred industrial supply partner for businesses seeking efficiency, reliability, and trust.

Our Partner

Addisons Paints & Chemicals

A Division of Simpson & Company Limited

As an authorized distributor of Addisons Paints & Chemicals, we're proud to bring their legacy of quality and innovation to our customers. Addisons is one of the oldest and most respected paint companies in India, established in 1948.

Part of the AMALGAMATIONS group and a division of Simpson & Company Limited, Addisons has become a significant global participant in the paint industry. They manufacture high-performance industrial and automotive coatings from their state-of-the-art facility in Chennai, serving major clients including Tata Steel, Jindal Steel, Wheels India Limited, and many other leading industrial companies.
